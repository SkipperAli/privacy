Privacy Policy

Gym Workout Tracker & Planner (shown on your phone as "Workout Tracker") · Developer: Skipper Labs · Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

The app keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. We never receive or see your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Training data: workouts, sessions, sets, weights and notes.
  • Nutrition data: foods, meals, calories, macros and water you log.
  • Body data: weight, tape measurements and body-fat figures.
  • Profile and settings: the age, sex, height, activity level and goal you enter to calculate targets, plus your app preferences.
  • Progress photos: only photos you choose to add. They are copied into the app's private storage.

Your training, nutrition and body logs are kept in an encrypted database. Your profile, settings and photos are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. None of it is uploaded, synced, sold or shared with anyone. Android's cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: workout, water and meal reminders that you control in Settings.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional): makes reminders and the rest timer fire on time.
  • Run at startup: re-arms your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: rest-timer alerts.
  • Network access and Google Play billing: added by the Google Play Billing library and used only to process the optional Pro purchase through Google Play. The app sends none of your data over the network.

Photos are picked with Android's system photo picker, so the app has no access to the rest of your gallery.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment or card details. The app only stores, on your phone, whether Pro is unlocked. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups and deleting your data

You can export an encrypted backup file yourself from Settings; where you keep it is up to you. The backup contains your logs but not your photos or settings. To delete everything, use Settings → Danger zone → Reset all data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Health information

Calorie, macro, body-fat and training suggestions are general estimates for fitness purposes, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training program, especially if you have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
